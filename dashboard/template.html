04 · Answers

What the answers look like

How each answer was scored, how often models are right, partly right or wrong, and the kinds of mistakes native speakers saw.

How every answer was scored

Rules handle exact matches, empty outputs, placeholders and explicit admissions of not knowing. Native speakers score the rest; where no valid human label exists, an LLM jury of two judges scores answers they agree on.

Right, partly right or wrong

Every scored answer gets 0 (wrong), 1 (admits not knowing), 2 (partly correct) or 3 (correct).

Kinds of answers

Every answer scored by rules or native speakers, by the type the scorer recorded. Types are descriptive: annotators agree on them less than on scores.

Source: Table 3 of the paper.

Cultural Riddles is written, checked and scored by native-speaker volunteers in the Cohere Labs Community. The data must not be used to train, fine-tune or distill models. Every number on this page is computed from the released data when the page is built.
